## Design Context

### Users
Desktop action-game players seeking immediate arcade control and a satisfying long-term roguelite progression loop. They play in focused 10–18 minute sessions with keyboard/mouse or a controller.

### Brand Personality
Electric, kinetic, triumphant. The product should make death feel like a charged tactical reset rather than failure.

### Aesthetic Direction
Solar-punk rave arcade planetarium: ember-toned planets against an ink-black void, sharp coral/mint/gold energy, deep layered particles, clear geometric enemy silhouettes, and intentional high-energy feedback. It must not resemble a generic neon dashboard, a wireframe prototype, or a retro-vector clone.

### Design Principles
1. Motion and effects explain player state, danger, and momentum.
2. The whole globe is the stage; surface movement must read at a glance.
3. Persistent progression is visible, desirable, and never cluttered.
4. Dense scenes preserve tactical readability through silhouette, telegraphs, and contrast.
5. Every screen earns its place in returning the player to a better next run.
